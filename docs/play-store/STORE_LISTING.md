# Cue Google Play Store Listing

## App name
Cue: Context Reminders

## Short description
Reminders that trigger when you arrive, get a call, open an app, or get notified.

## Full description
Cue helps you remember things at the moment they become relevant.

Create a reminder and choose what should trigger it:

- Arrive at or leave a place
- Receive a call from a person or phone number
- Open an app
- Receive a notification from an app, with optional text matching

Cue is designed to stay simple. There are no reminder titles to manage, no account to create, and no cloud dashboard. Create the reminder, choose the trigger, and Cue handles the rest.

PLACE REMINDERS
Search for an address, business, or place and choose the correct nearby result. Cue can remind you when you arrive or when you leave. Location reminders can work when Cue is closed when you choose to allow background location.

CALLER REMINDERS
Choose a contact or enter a phone number and have Cue show your reminder when that person calls. An optional caller banner can appear below the phone call controls so the reminder stays visible.

APP-OPEN REMINDERS
Choose an installed app and Cue can trigger your reminder when that app comes to the foreground. This feature uses Android Accessibility access only for foreground-app detection. Cue does not retrieve window contents and does not perform taps, gestures, typing, or other automated actions.

NOTIFICATION REMINDERS
Choose an app and optionally specify text to match. Cue can trigger your reminder when a matching notification arrives.

QUICK HOME-SCREEN WIDGET
Add Cue's Quick Reminder widget to your home screen for fast shortcuts to place, caller, app-open, and notification reminders.

PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN
Cue has no Cue account, advertising SDK, or Cue-operated cloud server. Reminder rules are stored locally on your device. You only enable the Android access needed for the trigger types you choose.

Some features require special Android access. Cue explains why each sensitive permission or service is needed before sending you to Android settings. See Cue's Privacy & data access page in the app for details.
