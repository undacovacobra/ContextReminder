Cue Privacy Policy

Effective: August 29, 2026

Cue is a context-based reminder application. Cue is designed to be local-first: it does not require a Cue account, contains no advertising SDK, contains no analytics SDK, and does not operate a Cue cloud service that receives your reminders or sensitive device data.

Data stored by Cue on your device

Cue may store reminder text, selected phone numbers or contact names, selected app package names, optional notification-matching text, saved place labels and coordinates, trigger settings, repeat settings, and reminder history needed to enforce cooldown or one-time rules. This information is stored locally on your device.

Location

Cue uses location in the background to trigger arrival and departure reminders you create, including when the app is closed or not in use. Saved place coordinates and reminder rules remain on your device. Cue does not operate a server that receives your location and does not use location for advertising. Android and Google system services may process device location, geofencing, or place-search requests according to your device settings and their own privacy terms.

Accessibility access

Cue uses Android Accessibility access only to detect when the package for an app you selected comes to the foreground, so Cue can trigger an app-open reminder you created. Cue does not retrieve other apps' window contents, does not read screen text through AccessibilityService, and does not perform taps, gestures, typing, or other automated actions. Detected package names are evaluated locally against your reminder rules.

Notification access

If you enable notification-trigger reminders, Cue may access the sending app, notification title, and notification text to evaluate rules you created. This evaluation occurs on your device. Cue does not send notification contents to a Cue-operated server.

Calls and phone numbers

If you enable caller reminders, Cue uses Android's call-screening role to detect the incoming phone number and compare it with caller reminders you created. Cue does not block, reject, record, or answer calls. Call information is not sent to a Cue-operated server.

Contacts

Cue does not request broad READ_CONTACTS access. When you choose a person for a caller reminder, Android's contact-selection interface grants Cue temporary access to the contact entry you selected so Cue can store the selected name and phone number locally.

Display over other apps

If enabled, Cue uses Android's display-over-other-apps capability only to show a caller-reminder banner where it will not be hidden by the phone call interface. Cue does not inspect or control other apps through this permission.

Sharing and sale of data

Cue does not sell personal data. Cue does not share reminder data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or a Cue-operated backend. Android and Google platform services used for location and place search operate under their own terms and device privacy settings.

Backups

Cue disables Android application backup for its private app data in the Play Store release configuration.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual reminders inside Cue. Clearing Cue's app data or uninstalling Cue removes Cue's locally stored reminder data. Cue does not create an online account, so there is no Cue cloud account to delete.

Children

Cue is a general-purpose productivity utility and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes

If Cue's data practices materially change, this policy and the in-app privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed behavior is released.

Contact

Support and privacy contact information should use the same developer contact shown on Cue's Google Play listing.
